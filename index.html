      First-time setup (5 minutes, once)
      1. Open the Firebase console → Firestore Database and click Create database. Pick a European location (e.g. eur3) and production mode. Stay on the free Spark plan.
      2. In Authentication click Get started, then under Sign-in method enable Google (choose your email as support email).
      3. In Authentication → Settings → Authorized domains, add fedcom7.github.io (localhost is there already).
      4. Press Sign in with Google here. Copy your UID, paste it into firestore.rules in place of PASTE_YOUR_UID, then paste the whole file into Firestore → Rules and publish.
      5. Press Sync now: your readings are uploaded and every device that signs in sees the same data.

      On your phone

      Open the link on your phone, then Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or Install app (Android). At home use the Wi-Fi address; anywhere else use the online version with Cloud sync.
